The Artist

Painting is how I pay attention.

Every canvas starts on location or from a memory worth keeping — a coastline at low tide, a bowl of citrus on the counter, a field that caught the light just right.

I work primarily in oil and acrylic, building up layers slowly until the painting feels true to the moment that inspired it.
