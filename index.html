The Artist

Painting is how I pay attention.

Every portrait starts with a character in mind — a tiny wizard, a cat curled up by the Christmas tree, a nod to a famous painting. Each one is painted on a small canvas, one whimsical idea at a time.

I work primarily in acrylic, building up layers slowly until the piece feels true to the character I imagined.
