About Ava

Hello, I'm Ava

A lifelong love of color and canvas.

I've been painting for as long as I can remember — starting with watercolors on the kitchen table and eventually finding my way to oil and acrylic on canvas. What began as a quiet hobby slowly grew into a full studio practice.

Today, my work centers on the landscapes, flowers, and everyday objects that catch my attention: the low light over a field at dusk, a bowl of citrus left on the counter, the particular blue of a coastline in early morning.

Each painting is made entirely by hand, from a blank canvas to a finished piece, using traditional brushes and a limited, considered palette.
