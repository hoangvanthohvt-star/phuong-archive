About Phuong

Hello, I'm Phuong

A lifelong love of color and canvas.

I've been painting for as long as I can remember — starting with watercolors on the kitchen table and eventually finding my way to acrylic on small canvas panels. What began as a quiet hobby slowly grew into a full studio practice.

Today, my work centers on whimsical cat portraits — a tiny wizard with his owl, a cat bundled up for the holidays, a playful nod to a famous painting. Every so often another animal or a landscape finds its way onto the easel too.

Each painting is made entirely by hand, from a blank canvas to a finished piece, using traditional brushes and a limited, considered palette.
